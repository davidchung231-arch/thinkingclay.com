## Preparing for a meeting with a U.S. professional

Write down the decision you need to make and a brief timeline. Share relevant background in advance, confirm the agenda and participants, and ask what materials the professional needs. During the meeting, note open questions, assigned tasks, and the next meeting or follow-up date.

## Organizing documents and questions before a business consultation

Gather the documents relevant to your question and label each with a clear date and description. Prepare a one-page summary, list your questions in priority order, and identify gaps in the information. Ask the professional how to transfer sensitive material securely before sending it.

## Managing communication and follow-up with U.S. service providers

Agree on a point of contact, preferred communication method, and next check-in. After a conversation, send a short summary of decisions, responsibilities, and agreed dates. Keep a shared record of open questions and follow up when an item needs clarification.

These guides provide general business coordination information, not legal advice. [Ask about business support](/contact/).
