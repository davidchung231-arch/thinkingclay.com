# Thinking Clay LLC

Thinking Clay operates as an outside smart employee — part of your team for the work that's too hard, too regulatory, or too cross-border to handle with existing headcount.

## What that means in practice

When a company needs FDA regulatory research for a new product category, we build the compliance map and walk you through it — not as a compliance vendor handing over a PDF, but as someone who understands how that regulation hits your actual operations.

When a seller needs to expand across borders or optimize an Amazon business, we embed enough to see the bottlenecks, then fix them alongside your team.

When a business needs legal structure, contract precision, or entity planning, David S. Chung, Esq. provides the strategy and drafting — but in coordination with the operational reality, not in isolation.

## Where we sit

We are not a law firm. We are not a traditional management consultant. We are a hybrid: an outside operator with legal and regulatory depth, hired to do the work that generalist staff can't.

## Who we work with

- Operators entering or expanding in the U.S. market
- Amazon sellers and e-commerce businesses 
- Companies navigating FDA, FTC, or cross-border regulatory requirements
- Teams that need interim research, strategy, or execution support without adding headcount
