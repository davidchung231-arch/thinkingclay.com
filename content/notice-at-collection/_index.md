## Notice at Collection

**Last updated: January 1, 2026**

This notice applies to California residents and complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

### Categories of Personal Information Collected

When you submit our contact form, we collect the following categories of personal information:

| Category | Examples Collected | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Identifiers** | Name, email address | To identify you and respond to your inquiry |
| **Customer Records** | Message content | To understand your needs and provide appropriate assistance |
| **Internet Activity** | None | This website does not collect IP addresses or browsing data |

### How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used for the following business purposes:

- To respond to your inquiry or message
- To provide the services or information you requested
- To follow up on our communication

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

### Third Parties with Whom We Share

We share your personal information only with the following categories of third parties:

- **Service Providers:** Formspree (formspree.io) — contact form submission processor
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law, subpoena, or other legal process

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

### Your California Privacy Rights

Under the CCPA and CPRA, you have the following rights:

#### Right to Know
You have the right to request disclosure of the personal information we collect, use, and share about you.

#### Right to Delete
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal retention requirements, completed transactions).

#### Right to Correct
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.

#### Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information
This website does not collect "sensitive personal information" as defined under CPRA.

#### Right to Non-Discrimination
We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights. This means we will not:
- Deny goods or services
- Charge different prices or rates
- Provide a different level or quality of goods or services

### How to Exercise Your Rights

To submit a request under the CCPA/CPRA, contact us at:

**Email:** hello@thinkingclay.com  
**Mailing Address:** Thinking Clay LLC, 12 Main St., Princeton, NJ 08540

We will respond to your request within **45 calendar days** of receipt. If we need additional time, we will notify you of the extension.

### Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. The agent must submit proof of authorization and verify their own identity. We may also require you to verify your identity directly.

### Do Not Sell My Personal Information

This website does **not** sell personal information. There is no opt-out link because no sale occurs.
