## A Practical Partner for Your U.S. Business

Thinking Clay helps businesses navigate operational complexity, improve communication, and turn practical ideas into action.

In the Greek myth, Ariadne's thread helps a traveler find a way through a labyrinth. It is a metaphor for our approach: understand the context, find the questions that matter, and follow a practical path forward.

We help clients prepare for conversations with U.S. professionals, organize documents and questions, coordinate follow-up, and improve everyday workflows. We consider each client's circumstances and budget when discussing the scope of work.

Thinking Clay LLC provides business advisory and coordination services. It does not provide legal services. Legal services are provided separately by David S. Chung, Esq. through his individual legal practice.
