An organized consultation packet helps a professional understand your situation and leaves more time for important questions. The right contents depend on the topic; ask what is needed before assembling a large collection.

## Prepare the information

1. **Write a brief overview.** Describe the business, the situation, the outcome you are considering, and known dates that affect planning.
2. **Select relevant documents.** Identify versions clearly, keep originals, and retain a list of what you shared.
3. **Create a timeline.** Put important events in order, distinguishing confirmed facts from assumptions and questions.
4. **Prioritize questions.** Start with the decision you face, then ask what information is missing and what the process involves.
5. **Agree on secure delivery.** Ask how to transmit sensitive information and who should have access before sending documents.

## Checklist

- One-page background and a short timeline.
- Clearly named, dated documents and a list of attachments.
- Questions in priority order, with uncertain facts marked.
- An agreed sharing method and a person responsible for follow-up.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not rely on file names such as “final” without dates or version details. Avoid sending every document without explaining its relevance. Identify uncertain or unfavorable facts so the professional can assess what further information is needed.
