<p class="attorney-lead">Practical legal guidance begins with understanding how a business actually operates.</p>

<p>David S. Chung, Esq. brings corporate legal experience and a business-oriented perspective to commercial legal work. His approach is direct and deliberate: clarify the question, identify the meaningful risks, and communicate the legal considerations in language business professionals can use.</p>

<p>That perspective is informed by a background in computer science and technology. It supports thoughtful use of AI and technology for research, organization, and workflow—always with legal judgment, confidentiality, and accuracy at the center.</p>

<p>The goal is a cost-conscious process built around clear scope, focused communication, and work that is proportionate to the decision in front of the business. No savings or outcome is promised; the emphasis is on clarity and sound process.</p>
